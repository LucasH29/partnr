Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Partnr ("we," "us," "our") connects high school students globally to find partners for startups, research, nonprofits, and competitions. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you create a Partnr account, we collect: your name, school email address, school name, country, city, grade level, languages spoken, and information you choose to share about your skills, interests, project goals, and target competitions. If you sign in using a school Google or Microsoft account, we also receive your name and email from that provider.

How we use this information

We use your information to match you with other students based on complementary roles, skills, and goals; to verify your school affiliation for community safety; and to display your profile to other students on the platform for matching purposes.

Who can see your information

Your profile (name, school, skills, and goals) is visible to other students using Partnr, since the platform's purpose is helping students find each other. We do not sell your personal information to third parties or use it for advertising.

Data storage

Your information is stored securely using Supabase, a database provider with industry-standard security practices. Emails are sent through Resend and Google Workspace.

Students under 18

Partnr is built for high school students, many of whom are minors. We collect only the information needed for the matching service to function, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information beyond what's described above. Parents or guardians with questions about their student's information can contact us at the email below.

Your choices

You can edit or delete the information in your profile at any time by signing in and using the Edit Profile option. You can request account deletion by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Partnr grows. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at hello@projectpartnr.com.
