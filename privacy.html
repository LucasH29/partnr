Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

2. How we use it

In short: To run Partnr, match you with partners, keep students safe and send you the emails you choose.
  • Create your account and profile, and check your school when possible.
  • Suggest partners and competitions (some of this uses AI; see section 4).
  • Let you message, post projects, apply and work with partners.
  • Send emails about requests, messages, applications and weekly matches. You can turn each type off in Email settings.
  • Keep Partnr safe: review reports, enforce our rules and stop misuse.
  • Fix bugs and improve Partnr.
  • We never sell your information, use it for advertising, or let advertisers target you.

3. Who can see your information

In short: Other students see your profile, never your email, birthday or zip code. You choose who can find you.
  • Other students can see your first name and last initial, city, grade, interests, skills, competitions, project idea, fun fact, links and experience.
  • You choose who can find you: students at your school, in your city, or anywhere. Your school name is shown only to students at your school and to partners you've connected with.
  • Never shown to other students: your email, birthday, zip code and email settings.
  • Messages and shared files are only seen by you and the partner you're talking with. We may review them if they're reported or if we believe someone is at risk.
  • Blocked students can't see you at all.
  • People who aren't signed in can't see any student profiles or projects.

5. How long we keep it

In short: As long as your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your data.
  • We keep your information while your account is open.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile, skills, projects, messages, files and settings within 30 days.
  • We may keep a small amount (like a report about someone who broke the rules) when we need it for safety or the law.

6. Your choices and rights

In short: You can see, change, download or delete your information, and turn off emails, anytime.
  • Edit your profile anytime from Edit profile.
  • Choose who can find you from the Matches page.
  • Turn off emails in Email settings, or tap Unsubscribe in any email.
  • Get a copy or delete everything: email hello@projectpartnr.com from your account's email. We'll reply within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have more privacy rights. We'll honor them.

7. Students under 18 and parents

In short: Partnr is for ages 13 to 19. Parents and guardians can ask to see, change or delete their teen's information.
  • Partnr doesn't allow anyone under 13. If we learn someone under 13 signed up, we delete the account.
  • If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must review our Terms and this policy with you.
  • A parent or guardian can email hello@projectpartnr.com to: see or download their teen's information, change their teen's privacy settings, or delete their teen's account. We'll confirm they're the parent or guardian first.
  • We don't collect precise location, don't show ads, and don't sell or share teens' information.
  • More tips are on our For Parents page.

8. How we protect it

In short: We use strong security and limit who can see what. No system is perfect.
  • Your data is stored with Supabase using encryption and strict access rules, so each student can only read their own private information.
  • Sign-in uses email links or trusted providers, so Partnr never stores passwords.
  • If a security problem ever affects your information, we'll tell you and the people we're required to tell.

9. Changes and contact

In short: We'll tell you before important changes. Questions go to hello@projectpartnr.com.
  • We'll update the date at the top when this policy changes, and tell you in the app or by email if the change is important.
  • Contact: hello@projectpartnr.com
