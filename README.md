# Partnr

**Find the right person to build with, at your school, in your city, or around the world.**

Partnr is a free web app that matches high school students with project partners by skills, interests and goals. Whether you're starting a business, launching a nonprofit, doing research or entering a competition, Partnr helps you find the teammate who has the skills you're missing.

**Try it:** [projectpartnr.com](https://projectpartnr.com)

---

## The Problem

Most ambitious students have the drive to build something real. What they're missing is the right partner.

Google Classroom and Discord help you work with people you already know. Paid mentors and research programs cost money most students don't have, and when the program ends, the help ends. No platform exists to find the right student partner, matched by skills and what you're actually trying to build, across schools, cities and countries.

Partnr is that platform.

## What Partnr Does

- **AI matching with real students.** Partnr reads your project idea, interests, skills and competitions, then picks the students who complement you and explains why you match. Every match is a real, registered student.
- **Complementary skills.** You're paired with someone whose strengths fill your gaps, not someone who does the same thing you do.
- **Find students.** Search for anyone by skill or competition, like "Python" or "DECA," at your school, near you, in your country or anywhere.
- **Open projects.** Post a project with the spots and skills you need, or apply to join someone else's.
- **250+ competitions.** Each one is tagged *Cross-school ok*, *Prep partner* or *Same school*, so you know what kind of partner you can team up with.
- **Deadlines.** A calendar of upcoming competition deadlines, checked automatically against official sources. Partnr never shows a guessed date.
- **My board.** A private planning space for your goals, the steps to reach them, saved partners and pinned competitions.
- **Inbox, chat and workspace.** Once a request is accepted, partners can message each other and share links and files.

## Built for Safety

Partnr is for high school students ages 13 to 19.

- **Nobody can message you until you accept their request.**
- **You choose who can find you:** only your school, your city or region, or anyone.
- **Limited profile info:** other students see your first name and last initial. Your email is never shown, and your school name is visible only to classmates and partners you've accepted.
- **School verification:** you can verify your school by sending a code from your school email.
- **Report and block** are on every profile and conversation.
- **Privacy is enforced in the database itself**, not just hidden on the screen.

## Built With

- **Front end:** HTML, CSS and JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Back end:** Supabase (PostgreSQL database, sign-in, row-level security and edge functions)
- **Email:** Resend
- **AI:** Claude API (Anthropic) for matching, competition suggestions and deadline checking

Partnr was designed, tested and built by a high school student, with Claude as an AI coding assistant.
